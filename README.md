## Series {#series}

### The Westeros Universe

All three shows are based on the books of __George R. R. Martin__:

- __Game of Thrones__ (2011–2019)
  - 8 seasons, 73 episodes on HBO
  - Noble houses fight for the ==Iron Throne== while an ancient threat rises in the North
- __House of the Dragon__ (2022–present)
  - Set about 200 years before _Game of Thrones_
  - Follows the civil war in House Targaryen, based on the book _Fire & Blood_
- __A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms__ (2026–present)
  - Set about 90 years before _Game of Thrones_
  - Follows the hedge knight Ser Duncan the Tall ("Dunk") and his squire Egg

> Watching order I recommend:
>
> > House of the Dragon → A Knight of the Seven Kingdoms → Game of Thrones

### Tron: Legacy (2010)

Sam Flynn is pulled into the digital world called _the Grid_. The soundtrack by __Daft Punk__ is one of my favorites.

If the Grid were code, entering it might look like this:

```python
def enter_the_grid(user):
    print(f"Welcome to the Grid, {user}.")
    return "Program initialized"

enter_the_grid("Sam Flynn")
```
